    How to play

    WASD or arrows to move. Crew: walk to your yellow task spots and hold E. Impostor: Q next to someone to kill (20 s cooldown). Anyone: R next to a body, or E at the red button in the cafeteria, calls a meeting. Vote someone out.

    Crew wins when every task is done or the impostor is voted out. The impostor wins when it equals the crew.
